02 · Reading Shelf

Seminal Literature & Papers

Essential books and research papers I am studying across software engineering, systems design, and artificial intelligence.

Systems Core · Book

Effective Modern C++

Scott Meyers

Mastering C++11 and C++14 nuances: universal references vs. rvalue references, perfect forwarding with std::forward, lambda captures, and smart pointer mechanics.

AI Foundation · Paper

Attention Is All You Need

Vaswani et al. (Google Brain)

The landmark paper introducing the Transformer architecture, eliminating recurrence, and proving multi-head self-attention as the foundation for modern LLMs.

Systems Architecture · Book

Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces

Remzi & Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau

Comprehensive breakdown of core OS concepts: CPU and memory virtualization, concurrent multithreading (locks, condition variables), and persistent file systems.

Distributed Systems · Book

Designing Data-Intensive Applications

Martin Kleppmann

The definitive guide to data models, storage engines (LSM-trees vs B-trees), replication, partition strategies, consensus algorithms, and fault-tolerant architecture.
